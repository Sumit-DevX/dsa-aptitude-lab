# Percentages — Meaning of Percent

## 1. What is a Percent?

The word **percent** literally means:

> **per hundred**

So, a percentage tells us **how many parts we have out of 100 equal parts**.

The symbol for percent is:

`%`

### Example

`25%` means:

> 25 out of every 100

So:

$$
25\% = \frac{25}{100}
$$

And:

$$
25\% = 0.25
$$`

---

## 2. Think of Percent as "Out of 100"

The easiest way to understand percentages is to imagine a group containing **100 items**.

For example:

- `1%` → 1 out of 100
- `10%` → 10 out of 100
- `25%` → 25 out of 100
- `50%` → 50 out of 100
- `75%` → 75 out of 100
- `100%` → 100 out of 100

Therefore:

$$
1\% = \frac{1}{100}
$$

$$
10\% = \frac{10}{100}
$$

$$
50\% = \frac{50}{100}
$$

$$
100\% = \frac{100}{100} = 1
$$

---

## 3. Percentage as a Fraction

A percentage can always be written as a fraction with **100 as the denominator**.

### Examples

$$
20\% = \frac{20}{100}
$$

$$
35\% = \frac{35}{100}
$$

$$
80\% = \frac{80}{100}
$$

The number before `%` becomes the numerator, while `100` becomes the denominator.

### General Rule

$$
x\% = \frac{x}{100}
$$

---

## 4. Percentage as a Decimal

Since a percentage means "out of 100", we can convert it into a decimal by dividing by `100`.

### Example

$$
25\% = \frac{25}{100} = 0.25
$$

Similarly:

$$
50\% = \frac{50}{100} = 0.5
$$

$$
75\% = \frac{75}{100} = 0.75
$$

### General Rule

$$
x\% = \frac{x}{100}
$$

Therefore, to convert a percent to a decimal:

> **Divide by 100.**

---

## 5. Understanding Common Percentages

Some percentages are particularly useful because they correspond to familiar fractions.

### 50%

`50%` means 50 out of 100.

$$
50\% = \frac{50}{100} = \frac{1}{2}
$$

So `50%` means **half**.

---

### 25%

$$
25\% = \frac{25}{100} = \frac{1}{4}
$$

So `25%` means **one quarter**.

---

### 75%

$$
75\% = \frac{75}{100} = \frac{3}{4}
$$

So `75%` means **three quarters**.

---

### 10%

$$
10\% = \frac{10}{100} = \frac{1}{10}
$$

So `10%` means **one tenth**.

---

### 100%

$$
100\% = \frac{100}{100} = 1
$$

So `100%` represents the **entire amount**.

---

## 6. Visualizing Percentages

Imagine a rectangle divided into **100 equal squares**.

If we shade:

- 10 squares → `10%`
- 25 squares → `25%`
- 50 squares → `50%`
- 75 squares → `75%`
- 100 squares → `100%`

The percentage tells us **what portion of the 100 squares is being represented**.

---

## 7. Percentage Does Not Always Mean the Number 100

An important idea:

> A percentage describes a **relationship to a whole**, not necessarily a group that literally contains 100 objects.

For example:

Suppose a class has 20 students and 5 of them are absent.

We can say:

> 25% of the students are absent.

Why?

Because:

$$
\frac{5}{20} = \frac{1}{4} = 25\%
$$

We use 100 as the standard reference point to describe the proportion.

---

## 8. The Big Idea

A percentage is simply another way of expressing a **fraction or proportion**, where the reference is always `100`.

For example:

$$
\frac{1}{2} = \frac{50}{100} = 50\%
$$

$$
\frac{1}{4} = \frac{25}{100} = 25\%
$$

$$
\frac{3}{4} = \frac{75}{100} = 75\%
$$

So:

> **Percent = a way of describing a proportion out of 100.**

# Quick Reference

| Percent | Fraction | Decimal | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `1%` | `1/100` | `0.01` | 1 out of 100 |
| `10%` | `10/100 = 1/10` | `0.10` | One tenth |
| `25%` | `25/100 = 1/4` | `0.25` | One quarter |
| `50%` | `50/100 = 1/2` | `0.50` | One half |
| `75%` | `75/100 = 3/4` | `0.75` | Three quarters |
| `100%` | `100/100 = 1` | `1.00` | The whole |

---

# Key Rules to Remember

### Percent → Fraction

Put the number over `100`.

$$
35\% = \frac{35}{100}
$$

### Percent → Decimal

Divide by `100`.

$$
35\% = 0.35
$$

### Decimal → Percent

Multiply by `100`.

$$
0.35 = 35\%
$$

---

# Mental Model

Whenever you see `%`, immediately think:

> **"out of 100"**

For example:

`40%`

Think:

$$
\frac{40}{100}
$$

which means:

> **40 parts for every 100 parts of the whole.**

This mental model is more important than memorizing conversion rules.
